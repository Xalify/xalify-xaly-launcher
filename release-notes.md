- **Logo art on every game page:** above the Install button, each game now shows its banner art from its itch.io page, which most games use for their logo. Games without a banner get a title logo drawn in the game's colours, with no more plain text box.
- **Real developer names:** the game page shows who made the game, read from the itch.io page's author list (including games made by several people). Until the page loads, Xaly shows a tidied-up version of the itch.io address, like "Grave Hour Studios" instead of "grave-hour-studios". Click the developer to see more of their games on itch.io.
