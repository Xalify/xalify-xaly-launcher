- **Find games on PC now finds itch.io games you downloaded yourself.** It looks in Downloads, Desktop, Documents, Program Files, Games folders on your drives and the itch.io app, and matches what it finds against the Store. Matches are added with the Store's name and cover art, linked to their Store page. Steam, Epic and GOG are no longer scanned.
- **Automatic:** every few hours, games from the Store that you downloaded outside Xaly (like a Super Smash Bros. Crusade folder in Downloads) are added to your library by themselves, with a notification. Only sure matches are added automatically; less certain ones wait in Find games on PC for you to tick. Games you remove are never added again. Turn it off in Settings › General.
- **Downloaded .zip files** of Store games can be unpacked and installed right from Find games on PC.
