- **Find games on your PC:** Library › **Find games on PC** looks in Steam, Epic Games, GOG, the itch.io app and any `Games` folder on your drives, plus folders you pick, and adds what you tick in one go. Steam games get their cover art. Steam and Epic games start through their own launcher, so Xaly can't count their play time.
- **Account backup:** Settings › Account › **Back up…** saves your library, play time, achievements, friends and settings into one password-protected file. Restore it on another PC from Settings or the sign-in screen (**Restore an account from a backup**). Games that aren't on the new PC show **Install** or **Find it…** when you open them.
- **Shortcuts:** any game's ⋯ menu can add a desktop shortcut or put it in the Start menu (under *Xaly Games*). The shortcut starts the game through Xaly, so play time still counts. Xaly itself gets a Start menu entry, and Settings › General has switches for a desktop and Start menu shortcut.
- **Uninstalling:** Xaly Launcher now appears in Windows Settings › Apps with a real uninstaller (also in Settings › About). It removes the launcher, its shortcuts and start-up entry, and lets you choose whether your games and accounts stay. Only Xaly's own files are deleted.
