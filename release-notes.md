- **Fixed: the game art no longer swaps pictures.** Opening a game used to show its cover art and then switch to another picture a moment later. Now a soft placeholder holds the spot while the game's itch.io page loads, and the final art appears once. If itch.io is slow, the cover art shows after 4 seconds. The art always takes the same space, so the buttons below don't jump.
- **Better logo picks:** a picture from a game's description is only used as its logo when it's the title image at the very top, before any text, and is a PNG, GIF or WebP image. Artwork and screenshots, like fight scenes, get the game's cover art instead.
